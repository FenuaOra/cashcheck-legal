CashCheck — Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

CashCheck keeps everything you enter on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in, and no bank connection. The app makes no network requests at all, so your figures are never sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

All of the following is held in the app's own private storage on your phone:

  • Your balance and the date it applies to
  • Income sources, recurring expenses, one-off items and repeating bills
  • Any ranges you set on those figures
  • Savings goals and saved comparison plans
  • Your balance check history
  • Preferences: currency symbol, pay day, theme and reminder settings

This data is included in an encrypted device backup if you use one, and it is deleted when you delete the app. Because there is no account, it cannot be recovered by us if the app or the device is lost. Keep your own copy if the history matters to you — see Exporting your data below.

What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you choose to send it. CashCheck contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs. It does not request network access, and there is no server for it to contact.

Exporting your data

Settings offers Export a copy, which creates a CSV file of your figures and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where that file goes is entirely your choice — Files, Mail, another app. Once it leaves CashCheck it is outside the app's control and this policy, and it is readable by anything that opens it. The export contains your financial entries, so treat it as you would a bank statement.

Reminders

If you turn on the monthly balance check or forecast drift reminders, CashCheck asks iOS for permission to show notifications and schedules them locally on the device. No reminder is sent through a server, and no push token is created or transmitted.

Tracking

CashCheck does not track you. It collects no identifiers, does not follow you across apps or websites, and shows no advertising. Its privacy manifest declares no collected data types.

Children

CashCheck is a general-audience planning tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

What CashCheck is not

CashCheck is a calculator, not an adviser. It projects your balance from the figures you type in and cannot know about anything you have not told it. The best, likely and worst cases come from ranges you set yourself; they are not odds or confidence levels. Nothing in the app is financial, investment, tax or legal advice, and nothing it shows is a recommendation.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published here with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
