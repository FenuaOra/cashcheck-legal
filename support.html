Common questions

Can CashCheck connect to my bank?

No, and it is not planned. Every figure is entered by you. That is why the app needs no account and no sign-in, and why nothing you enter leaves your phone.

What do the best, likely and worst cases mean?

They come from the ranges you set on your own income and expenses. Tap ± on any item to give it a low and a high. The band widens the further ahead you look, because month-to-month variation partly cancels out rather than stacking up. They show how wide the outcome could be — they are not odds, and CashCheck does not work out how likely any of them is.

Why doesn't my balance match the forecast?

That is what the monthly balance check is for. Enter what you actually hold and CashCheck compares it with what it expected. If you come in under, it says by how much and what your real saving rate was. One short month is normal; two in a row means the figures need revising.

Does changing the currency convert my figures?

No. The currency setting changes the symbol shown next to your numbers and nothing else. There is no exchange rate and no conversion.

I cleared everything but my currency stayed the same

That is deliberate. Start over and clear everything clears your figures — balance, income, expenses, one-offs, goals and balance history. It leaves your preferences alone, so you do not have to re-pick your currency, pay day and theme every time you start again.

How do I get my data out?

Settings → Export a copy produces a CSV file and opens the share sheet, so you can save it to Files or send it to yourself. The file contains your financial entries, so treat it as you would a bank statement.

Will I lose my data if I delete the app?

Yes. Everything is stored on the device and there is no account to restore from, so deleting the app deletes your figures. An encrypted device backup includes them; an export is the simplest way to keep your own copy.

My reminders aren't arriving

Check iOS Settings → Notifications → CashCheck and confirm notifications are allowed. The reminders are scheduled locally on your phone, so they also need the relevant toggle switched on inside CashCheck's own Settings screen.

Reporting a problem

Useful things to include:

  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead
  • The screen you were on
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • A screenshot, if the problem is something you can see

Please do not send your exported CSV unless it is genuinely needed — it contains your financial entries, and a description of the problem is usually enough.
